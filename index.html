About

Slim Allouche is a director of photography and editor working primarily in music video and artist film. His approach favors natural light, tight crews, and a color palette that leans into contrast rather than flattening it — treating every video as a short film first, a promo second. Recent work includes videos for Ramsjamsss, Cosmooo, Von Moon and LGX. Available for music video, narrative, and commercial bookings.
